# Exploratory inspection regimes



This is a declared exploratory grid of workload, state time, inspection duration, noise, cost and peak headroom. All cells, including unfavourable ones, are exported.

It uses diagnostic validation scenario 10011 and a wide initial health prior [0.2,1], not the main experiment prior [0.6,1]. These are model assumptions, not measurements.

Completed 96 cells. Inspection had lower mean truncated cost than both alternatives in 33 cells. Both upper paired bounds were below zero in 0 cells.

These are Monte Carlo diagnostic intervals, with no correction for scanning many cells. A favourable cell is a hypothesis for independent confirmation, not a statistically established discovery, RL improvement, or evidence of practical savings.

The 180-minute estimate has no residual value. Longer-horizon policy experiments and model-mismatch checks are needed. No cell has been omitted because it was unfavourable.
